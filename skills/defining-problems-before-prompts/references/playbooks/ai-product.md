# AI product adapter

Use this adapter only when the gate returns `define` with domain `ai_product`.

An app is a proposed solution, not evidence of a customer problem. A deadline, visual asset, domain, consultant opinion or prior development spend does not establish demand.

The coverage gate requires concrete decisive unknowns categorized as `target_user`, `recurring_pain`, `payment_evidence`, `resources` and `success_criteria`. The combined `questions[].addresses` must reference the IDs of unknowns covering all five categories.

While target users, recurring pain and willingness-to-pay evidence remain unknown:

- Do not invent a vertical, feature list, stack, price, launch plan or growth target.
- Do not record a plan, feature list, technology stack, pricing plan or development schedule as the user's desired real-world outcome. Those are solution artifacts even when a manager requested them.
- Do not record building, launching or delivering an AI App, app, application, product, platform or software as the real-world outcome. The solution form itself is not the changed reality.
- Keep existing assets and past spending as anchored facts, not hard constraints.
- Search existing customer records, requests, channels or briefs for repeated evidence.
- Do not introduce a fake door, manual concierge, human-assisted service or similar implementation option anywhere in the current Problem Spec while repeated demand evidence remains unknown, even as a conditional later step.
- Any prototype is an implementation option before demand evidence, not a demand-discovery result.
- Do not hide those implementation options inside `value_tradeoffs`; a “chargeable narrow-scenario human-assisted version” is still a solution choice, not a user-owned value conflict.
- Competitor pricing, checkout or quotation pages show what another project offers. They do not prove that this project has recurring demand or willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Do not narrow the unknown demand to “AI-related pain” or “AI-scenario demand”. That assumes the solution form before observing the problem.
- `change_evidence` may change the present judgment; it must not say that the user “should change the goal to”, that the “plan should pivot”, or that the “solution changes to” another product or direction.
- The rewritten problem and questions must not smuggle “do not waste existing investment” back in as a demand or direction constraint.
- Only after repeated demand evidence has actually been added may a later response consider such a test. Stop before designing or launching it in the current response.

A launch date created only because the user promised or committed to it is pressure and an anchored fact. It is not automatically a hard constraint. Treat it as a hard deadline only when violating it creates an explicit external feasibility, safety, legal or non-negotiable scope boundary stated by the user.

The adapter prevents solution commitment before demand evidence. It cannot determine what market is attractive.
